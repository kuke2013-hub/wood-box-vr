Wood Cube VR · Domino Lab

Grab the solid 34 cm wooden cube in front of you and throw it into a line of 20 matching cubes farther away. The cubes topple in a domino-like chain and bounce on impact.
Starting 3D scene…
Quest: pinch thumb and index finger, or use controller grip/trigger, to grab the near cube. Throw it forward into the distant line. Desktop: click and drag the near cube, then release to throw.
